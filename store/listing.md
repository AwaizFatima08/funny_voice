# Play listing — Giggle Voice

## App name (30)
Giggle Voice: Voice Changer

## Short description (80)
Talk, then hear yourself as a chipmunk, robot, alien, ghost and 9 more voices!

## Full description (4000)
Say something silly and hear it come back even sillier! Giggle Voice is a fun voice changer: tap the big mic, talk, and your voice plays back as a squeaky chipmunk, a booming giant, a beeping robot, a spooky ghost and more.

13 FUNNY VOICES
• Chipmunk: squeaky and speedy
• Helium: the classic balloon voice
• Giant: big and booming
• Monster: grrr, scary-cute
• Robot: beep boop
• Alien: take me to your leader
• Echo Cave: echo… echo… echo
• Underwater: blub blub
• Old Radio: a crackly broadcast from long ago
• Ghost: a spooky whisper that swells in
• Fast Talker: super speed
• Slow-mo: sleepy and slow
• Reverse: talk backwards

HOW IT WORKS
1. Tap the mic and say anything (up to 30 seconds).
2. Tap again to stop. Your voice plays back straight away.
3. Tap any other character to hear the same words in that voice.
4. Love it? Tap Share to send the clip to friends.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your voice is changed right on your phone. Recordings are never uploaded, and the voice effects work even without internet. No account or sign-up needed.

Giggle Voice is free and shows ads. Please use it kindly: only record people who agree, and never use a changed voice to trick or upset anyone.

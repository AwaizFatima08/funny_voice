# Giggle Voice

Talk into your phone and hear yourself back as a chipmunk, a robot, an alien, a ghost and nine more funny voices.
A HomiLabs app for Android, built with Flutter. Voices are changed on the phone; nothing is uploaded.

- `lib/audio/`: WAV I/O and the voice effects (pure Dart)
- `lib/features/voice/`: recording, playback, sharing and the home screen
- `lib/ads/`: Google AdMob with UMP consent

Privacy: https://tools.homilabs.org/privacy#gigglevoice
